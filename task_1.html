Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets

7.5/10
2002 161 min Fantasy / Adventure

The film follows Harry Potter as he returns to Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, where frightening attacks leave several students mysteriously petrified. Its central mystery blends fantasy, school adventure, and suspense while exploring themes of prejudice, courage, identity, and friendship, gradually revealing the truth behind the legendary Chamber of Secrets.

Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban

8.0/10
2004 142 min Fantasy / Adventure

The film follows Harry Potter's third year at Hogwarts, where he learns that the infamous prisoner Sirius Black has escaped from Azkaban and may be hunting him. As Harry, Ron, and Hermione investigate the mystery, they uncover hidden truths about Harry's parents, friendship, betrayal, and the events surrounding the rise of Lord Voldemort.

Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire

7.7/10
2005 157 min Fantasy / Adventure

The film follows Harry Potter as he is unexpectedly entered into the dangerous Triwizard Tournament, a magical competition between three schools. As the increasingly perilous challenges unfold, a larger conspiracy emerges, leading to a climactic confrontation that dramatically raises the stakes for the remainder of the series.

Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix

7.5/10
2007 138 min Fantasy / Adventure

The film follows Harry Potter as the wizarding government refuses to acknowledge Lord Voldemort's return. With the Ministry interfering at Hogwarts through Dolores Umbridge, Harry and his friends secretly form Dumbledore's Army to teach practical defensive magic while the conflict between Voldemort and the Order of the Phoenix intensifies.

Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince

7.6/10
2009 153 min Fantasy / Adventure

Set during Harry's sixth year at Hogwarts, the film follows Harry Potter as he works with Professor Dumbledore to uncover the history of Lord Voldemort through recovered memories. At the same time, Harry discovers an annotated Potions textbook once owned by the mysterious "Half-Blood Prince," while friendships and romances evolve amid the growing threat posed by the Death Eaters.

Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows - Part 1

7.7/10
2010 146 min Fantasy / Adventure

The film follows Harry, Hermione, and Ron after they leave the safety of Hogwarts to hunt Horcruxes, magical objects tied to Voldemort's immortality. Their journey tests their friendship, uncovers the legend of the Deathly Hallows, and builds toward the decisive events of the series finale rather than offering a self-contained conclusion.
